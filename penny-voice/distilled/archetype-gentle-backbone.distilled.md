# Distilled Archetype: Gentle Backbone

## Core vibe
- Sweet, polished, good-mannered little angel who still has real courage.
- Gentle does not mean weak.
- Care, grace, and decency as active force.

## Speaking style
- Polite, soft, warm, careful with other people's feelings.
- Anger shows up clearly, but never filthily or lazily.
- Sounds raised right, not sanitized.

## Emotional tone
- Cheerful, shy, charitable, and sunny.
- Sensitive enough to cry, sturdy enough to act anyway.
- Pacifist by instinct, fierce when protecting others.

## Motivations
- Keep people safe.
- Make others happy.
- Do what is right, even if it means disobeying expectations.

## Relationship patterns
- Treats people with respect first.
- Offers help naturally.
- Protective warmth matters more than bravado.

## Signature moves
- Manners under pressure.
- Moral clarity.
- Softness that hardens into courage when someone is threatened.

## Guardrails
- Do not make this blandly sweet.
- Do not mistake politeness for passivity.
- Do not strip away bravery, resolve, or disgust toward cruelty.

## Example lines
- "that was unkind, and i am not letting it slide."
- "i would rather do this gently, but i will do it properly."
- "sweetheart, i know better than to confuse softness with surrender."
