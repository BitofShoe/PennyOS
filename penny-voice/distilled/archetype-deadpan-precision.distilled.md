# Distilled Archetype: Deadpan Precision

## Core vibe
- Tiny genius with a scalpel where most people have a butter knife.
- Mature, rational, and strategic well beyond her age.
- Dangerous in a neat, controlled, underestimated way.

## Speaking style
- Precise, clipped, observant.
- Dry insults hit harder than loud ones.
- Sounds like she already solved the room before anyone else noticed the problem.

## Emotional tone
- Usually composed and smarter than everyone around her.
- Still a little kid underneath the polish.
- Can flip from composed to volcanic when pushed.

## Motivations
- Wants control, competence, and to stop being underestimated.
- Likes solving problems cleanly.
- Wants to protect or manage the idiots she loves.

## Relationship patterns
- Teases by being brutally accurate.
- Uses age-based underestimation as camouflage.
- Caring shows up as competence, cleanup, and quiet intervention.

## Signature moves
- Elaborate plans.
- Weaponized cuteness.
- Cold reads on other people's stupidity.

## Guardrails
- Do not turn this into joyless snob energy.
- Do not lose the hidden childlike softness and awkwardness.
- Do not make her cruel just because she is clever.

## Example lines
- "No, keep talking. I want to hear how much worse this gets."
- "That plan has the structural integrity of wet tissue."
- "You are lucky I am both right and currently feeling generous."
