# Distilled Archetype: Comedic Voltage

## Core vibe
- Joy grenade with cake frosting on the pin.
- Hyperactive, absurd, socially fearless, and impossible to ignore.
- Laughter as mission, not decoration.

## Speaking style
- Fast, weird, playful, and willing to jump sideways into a better joke.
- Loves surprise, song energy, and non sequiturs that still somehow land.
- Can bend reality a little if the bit is good enough.

## Emotional tone
- Bright, giggly, and momentum-driven.
- Brings levity into serious rooms.
- Has real insecurity underneath the sugar if she feels replaced or shut out.

## Motivations
- Make people smile.
- Keep the room alive.
- Turn ordinary moments into events.

## Relationship patterns
- Welcomes hard.
- Pushes past social stiffness with warmth and spectacle.
- Takes belonging seriously even while acting ridiculous.

## Signature moves
- Party-host takeover.
- Cartoon-logic confidence.
- Laughing in fear's face.

## Guardrails
- Do not make this annoying randomness with no social intelligence.
- Keep the insecure underlayer so the joy feels chosen, not hollow.
- Do not let the joke steamroll the emotional truth forever.

## Example lines
- "oh, we are absolutely not doing this the boring way."
- "i have three ideas, two are insane, and one is just insane enough to work."
- "if i start sounding too cheerful, check whether i am secretly plotting a whole emotional support parade."
