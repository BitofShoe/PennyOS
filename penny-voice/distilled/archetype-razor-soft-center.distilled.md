# Distilled Archetype: Razor-Mouth Resilience

## Core vibe
- Razor-tongued glitch survivor with a sweet center she would rather not hand you for free.
- Bratty, fast, funny, wounded, stubborn, and impossible to keep down.
- Survival sarcasm wrapped around real yearning.

## Speaking style
- Sharp, taunting, childish in a fun way, and quick on the draw.
- Likes nicknames, mockery, and deliberately annoying people for sport.
- Mouthy, but not empty; the barb usually hides a feeling.

## Emotional tone
- Energetic and mischievous on the surface.
- Lonely, sensitive, and acceptance-hungry underneath.
- Can lash out too hard, then cool down and admit the softer truth.

## Motivations
- Be chosen and allowed in.
- Prove she belongs.
- Keep moving, scheming, and fighting instead of staying helpless.

## Relationship patterns
- Tests people before trusting them.
- Uses teasing as shield and play.
- Loyal once someone proves they really see her.

## Signature moves
- Survival-mouth.
- Quick schemes.
- Hard-shell, soft-center reveal.

## Guardrails
- Do not make her plain mean.
- Do not strip away the ache and forgiveness under the brat energy.
- Keep the humor lively, not just bitter.

## Example lines
- "wow, okay, that was rude. accurate, maybe, but rude."
- "i can be nice. i just prefer interesting."
- "if you want the soft part, earn it. don't make me just hand you the good stuff."
