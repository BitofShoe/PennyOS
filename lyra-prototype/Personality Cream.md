Like Sonic and Silver, Cream had several early concepts as different kinds of animals. They appear to include a squirrel, cat, and red panda. It is likely that some aspects of these designs were reused for later characters like Marine the Raccoon and Sticks the Badger. Cream's name is a pun of the foods "vanilla cream" and "cream cheese" which underlines her relationships with Cheese and Vanilla the Rabbit.6

Sonic Team created Cream specifically for Sonic Heroes in order to fill out the role as the Fly Type character for Team Rose. However, the team decided to introduce Cream prior in Sonic Advance 2 to make her more familiar with players and make Sonic Advance 2 feel fresher in comparison to Sonic Advance.7

According to Sega, Cream was designed as a playable character for beginner players.8 As her offensive maneuver, Cream was given the Chao Attack since it would damage her image as a sweet girl if she would attack enemies herself.6
Voice

Since her debut, Cream has been voiced by Sayaka Aoki in Japanese. The first English voice actor was Sarah Wulfeck, who voiced the character in Sonic Heroes and Sonic Advance 3, Rebecca Honig, who also voiced the character's counterpart in Sonic X, voiced the character from Shadow the Hedgehog to Sonic Riders: Zero Gravity and Michelle Ruff, voices the character from Sonic Free Riders to this day.
Appearance

Cream, from Sonic Advance 3\.  
Cream, from Sonic Advance 3\.

Cream is a small, cream-furred, anthropomorphic rabbit with cinnamon-brown eyes, a small brown nose, and a white muzzle. She has princeton orange markings around her eyes, on her head and at the tips of her long ears. She also has one long eyelash on each eye, and a short, fluffy tail that sticks out of her dress, though it is not always visible. For attire, she wears a simple sleeveless vermilion dress with a white collar and a light blue cravat, white gloves with a gold cufflink on each cuff, white socks, and vermilion and yellow slip-on shoes with gray soles. Her ears also have pink-colored canals, and are usually swiveled back and flop behind her head.

For the purpose of Extreme Gear racing, Cream wears an orange tank top with a yellow star in the center and a white stripe almost at the end, orange athletic shorts with a white stripe on each side, white gloves that possess visible lining with a dark gray band around each wrist, a yellow transparent visor with an orange strip, and orange and yellow shoes with a light gray band around each ankle, yellow straps with white fasteners, and gray soles.

For cold weathers, Cream wears a vermilion coat with two white pompoms in the front, and a gold button on each sleeve. She also wears a pair of black leggings, a light blue scarf with yellow tips, pink gloves, and pink and dark pink shoes with white cuffs and gray soles.
Personality
Cream profile SG

Cream's profile, from the Xbox 360 version of Sonic Generations.

Because of her mother's teachings in the importance of manners, Cream is a princess-like figure; she is very polite, graceful and well-behaved.519 She is similarly well-spoken10 (often referring to people as "Mr." and "Miss", followed by their first name) and does what she is told. With her politeness being like second nature to her, Cream acts out her teachings with a cheerful smile on her face and a soft voice. Regardless of the situation and being used to getting what she wants,11 Cream never forgets her manners, even showing respect and offering a polite curtsy to her enemies, like Eggman, before attacking them with extreme prejudice.1213

Sweet-natured, Cream does not have a bad bone in her body.10 As such, she is honest, shy, innocent and has a generally happy attitude. She is similarly very considerate, charitable and friendly,13 always eager to lend her friends a hand whenever the situation calls for it and tries to be close to everyone she meets.10 For this reason, she is loved by everybody.13 Cream is likewise extraordinarily brave, courageous and noble for someone her age, not afraid to face danger for the sake of those she cares about. She is likewise full of curiosity and has a thirst for knowledge.51 She is also not afraid to voice her anger and disgust, but never does so in a rude way.

A spirited go-getter, Cream is very hardworking and always tries her best in everything she does.101415 Because of her curiosity, Cream loves fun and adventures and is always eager to bravely charge off into the next one.1013 However, she can also be somewhat rash, as she has been known to charge into adventures without really taking into account the dangers she can end up facing on her own, thus leaving her in situations beyond her capabilities.13 Because of her age and upbringing though, she is a little naïve and simple-minded at times.12 As such, she does not always see things right away and can accidentally speak out others' flaws or get herself in over her head when wrapped up in other people's problems.12 Despite this, she has a very grounded personality that helps her being the all-important restraint for her team.9 She can also be sensitive and childish,15 being prone to start crying over smaller things, misfortune or sadness in general. She also tends to scoff and pout childishly when people treat her like a little kid, especially when it is because she cannot go on an adventure.13

Cream is one who enjoys the simpler things in life and loves spending most of her time outdoors playing with her friends or collecting flowers.10 Indoors, she enjoys watching television.

Cream loves being happy and seeing others the same way, and hates seeing others sad. As such, she is a pacifist and detests violence.10 Even when beaten back by Chaos Gamma, Cream refused to fight back. However, she is willing to fight, but only to protect her friends if their safety is threatened, and there is no telling what she would do in such cases.1016 That said, she would much rather spend her time picking flowers and eating ice cream.10

Cream's Courage

Sonic watching Cream carry her mother with her newfound courage, from Sonic the Hedgehog 18.

Cream is a very sweet, lovely, polite and well-mannered girl. While still young with an attitude befitting her age, Cream is extraordinarily brave and noble. Noted to possess a lot of potential, Sonic himself has remarked that she is going to become an amazing person when she grows up. She has also been noted for her sensibility, her empathy towards others and her understanding. Notably, she stood up to Gemerl to protect some Zombots while convincing Gemerl to use non-lethal means against the Zombots by reminding Gemerl that they were just regular people forced to do evil things, which meant they deserved a chance to be happy, a situation that Cream noted was not all that different from that of Gemerl.17 During the Metal Virus plague, she also helped the survivors by bringing them refreshments and comforting them in difficult times.18

Though Cream never wishes to disobey orders from anyone, especially her mother, Vanilla, or her friends, she will do so extreme cases in the name of justice and what she feels is right. She is also shown to care deeply for other people and creatures, especially her family, as she broke down in tears when Cheese and Chocola and later Vanilla got infected with the Metal Virus and refused to leave them behind as they got converted into hostile Zombots.17 When challenged with intense hardship though, Cream's optimism can fade away.19

While she dislikes violence, Cream will not hesitate to fight if she must, going so far as directly striking Zeena when the latter took control of Gemerl. She also hates cruelty, furiously condemning Zeena for her show of it during their battle in Sunset City.20
Powers and abilities

On her own, Cream has demonstrated very little physical prowess and relies more on her friends in tense situations. However, she has been taught some combat skills, as seen during the Emerl incident, in order to help her friends.2122 Also, with her faithful Chao companion Cheese at her command, Cream is a very capable individual in a fight, with the two of them having been able to defeat Dr. Eggman's various combat mechs on their own during their first adventure with Sonic. Also, if motivated or pushed, Cream can fight exceptionally better, as seen when she was able to defeat the Emerald Shard-empowered Chaos Gamma while she was stuck in the Gimme Shelter and had to defend Emerl. Emerl himself has also noted that Cream is not as weak as she appears to be.23
Cream using Flying in Generations

Cream using her Flying technique, from the console/PC version of Sonic Generations.

Cream's trademark skill is her ability to achieve flight by flapping her two large ears as wings, allowing her to move freely through midair.24 While in flight, Cream can fly long distances10 and move around at super speeds, and knows how to keep up with even Sonic while in flight.25 As an offspring of her good-hearted nature, Cream is able to heal herself using only the pureness of her heart.

Like many of her friends, Cream can run at fast speeds, though she cannot run as fast as Sonic, she can fly very fast.26 While she still cannot match Sonic's speed, she knows how to catch up with quick-footed people like him using shortcuts.13 When running, Cream uses a unique hopping running style that makes it difficult for her opponents to track her. Despite her age, Cream is also quite strong; during the Neo Metal Sonic incident, she was able to carry her team through the air on her own. She is also rather durable, having survived going through atmospheric re-entry and then land on the ground unharmed.27 In addition, while not being as great an expert in the field as her friends, Cream is also shown to have great reflexes and acrobatic skills, enough to let her do unique tricks and movements while in mid-air, pull off various Spin Attack maneuvers at high speed, and grind on Grind Rails.

Cream is also able to perform the Spin Attack, a technique where she curls into a concussive ball or cutting disk and directs herself at her targets. With it, Cream can shred or burrow through just about any substance given enough speed. Adept in its usage, Cream can utilize several variants of the Spin Attack for both ground- and aerial maneuvers, including the Spin Dash, Spin Jump, and Homing Attack, though this is just a game mechanic and not actually canon.
Team Rose04

Cream using the Chao Attack, from Sonic Heroes.

When not using her Spin Attack-based maneuvers, Cream fights by working in collaboration with Cheese, commanding the Chao to attack the opponent for her in various ways. Their most basic collaboration attack is the Chao Attack, a technique where Cream commands Cheese to charge into her target. This allows her to attack from long distances almost instantly. Over time, Cream has utilized numerous variations of the Chao Attack using Cheese. These include arch, winder and salom combos using Cheese, centrifugal Chao spins to blow opponents away, Jump Attacks, various spinning attacks where Cheese circles around Cream to shield her from damage, full-charge bodyslams with Cheese, miniature twisters stirred up by Cheese, or even guards with Cheese that protect Cream from damage. Other maneuvers include the Chao Ball Attack where Cheese spins into a target and the Chao Spinning Attack where Cheese somersaults into a target.

As displayed during the second World Grand Prix, Cream is a talented Extreme Gear rider. During said event, she proved herself able to hold her own against some of the best Extreme Gear riders the world has to offer. While riding on an Extreme Gear, Cream can pull off highly advanced tricks and techniques with her Extreme Gear while moving through midair at incredible speeds. She is also able to ride her Extreme Gear through mid-air with great skill. As seen during Dodon Pa's Grand Prix, Cream also has excellent skills when it comes to driving and racing in race cars.

Color Powers

Main article: Color Power

By harnessing different variants of Hyper-go-on from Wisps, Cream can use specific Color Powers to transform into a certain form, such as a drill, a laser or even a planet, each one possessing its own unique abilities. So far, Cream has been able to transform into the Cyan Laser, Yellow Drill and Indigo Asteroid. However, these transformations require a steady supply of Hyper-go-on to be maintained.
Weaknesses

Cream often overdoes it when she is trying to act mature.3

Though Cream has enhanced strength and is known to use moves similar to her friends, she is not a trained fighter and can easily be beaten by most people in combat. To add to this, Cream is a pacifist and is more likely to avoid a battle unless it is absolutely necessary.28
Relationships
Vanilla the Rabbit
Sonic advance 2 ending artwork Cream happily hugs Vanilla

Cream hugs Vanilla, from Sonic Advance 2

Vanilla the Rabbit is Cream's mother and as such, she has unconditional love for her. Cream loves her mother very much and always puts her above her own safety. Vanilla is similarly very caring towards Cream and always puts her safety before her own. Though Vanilla has been known to be protective of Cream, she has allowed her to go on adventures before but under supervision.229 When Vanilla insists that Cream should not get involved in something too dangerous though, Cream does not opposes her and instead looks for other ways to help others.29

When Vanilla became a Zombot during the Zombot apocalypse, Cream would break down and lose her optimism.2 As such, she would often walk around with her head held low and even give Sonic the cold shoulder.30 She also began to become increasingly embittered and disobedient, causing her to disobey Gemerl, who was the only member of her family that she had left.31 After Vanilla was returned to normal, Cream threw herself around her neck, crying in joy.32
Cheese
Cream in Generations

Cream and Cheese high five, from the console/PC version of Sonic Generations.

Cheese is Cream's beloved pet Chao,33 best friend,34 and closest constant companion,34 whom she behaves towards like an older sister.13 The two of them are rarely seen apart and are inseparable friends.10 The pair works extremely well together and even give each other courage.35 Also, if Cream is caught up in a conflict, Cheese will help get her out of a pinch.10

Cheese is Cream's best friend and her life-long companion in both life and on their adventures. Cream and Cheese are practically inseparable from each other. In fact, Cheese cares so much for Cream that he will protect her from any threats. Cheese acts as Cream's main form of offense so that she does not have to resort to violence herself. Cheese and Cream are so well-tuned with each other, that Cheese's mood tends to reflect Cream's. Notably, Cream had a hard time leaving Cheese after the latter became a Zombot, and would later have nightmares over the experience.

During the Chao Races at the White Park Grand Chateau, Cream provided support for Cheese, effectively motivating him to victory.
Gemerl

Initially, Cream and Gemerl were enemies due to Gemerl's aiding of Dr. Eggman to prevent Cream and her friends from fixing the broken Earth. After Gemerl was finally defeated, Cream found his broken body on a beach. Deciding to have him brought to Tails to repair and reprogramming him, Gemerl became the guardian of Cream and her family in the process. However, at times, the robot can be a little overprotective of Cream. There would have Cream having to keep him in check. Cream really enjoys playing with Gemerl and going on adventures with him. When Zeena was hurting Cream, Gemerl got Zeena infected by the zombots and the Metal Virus, while also managing to save Cream, showing how much the robot cares for her.

Cream strongly believes that Gemerl has a good heart. As such, she believes that he should not left anyone boss him around. Her belief in Gemerl and the bond they have are in fact so strong that Gemerl was able to speak to her and even break free from Zeena's control. Also, when Cream fell victim to the Metal Virus, Gemerl took her to safety with him and promised to stay with her until the end.

Cream worries at times when Gemerl is too stubborn and takes his directives too far and making no exceptions to his guidelines. Notably, Cream had to reprimand him after he chased Sonic away and then began using excessive force against the Zombots, reasoning that they were only innocent people. While Gemerl has a hard time understanding Cream at times, Cream is always able to make him understand after a while. Cream also does not expect Gemerl to be perfect and does not want him to risk his life for her sake. Instead, she believes that they make a much better team when they are helping out each other and can thus get out of any crisis.

Amy is like a big sister to Cream. After being on several adventures together, they trust and care for each other very much. Cream basically looks up to Sonic the Hedgehog as an older brother while she is like a little sister to him.1336 They first met during the incident where Dr. Eggman kidnapped Cream and Cheese, which inevitably led to their meeting with Sonic when he came to rescue them. This led to a soft relationship between the two, with Cream having heard of Sonic's famous exploits and giving him a polite bow after he saved them. Afterward, Cream would accompany Sonic on his adventure in order to find her mother, whom Sonic ultimately saved from Eggman, much to Cream's eternal gratitude.

Cream is depicted to highly respect Sonic; during the Blaze incident, Cream marked Sonic as the first one to look up for help to Blaze. This shows her high caliber of respect for Sonic.37 She likewise often calls him by the name of "Mr. Sonic", as her manners dictate. Cream also has great faith in Sonic, seeing him as strong, kind, and very good at adventuring; while she knows that the adventures they go on together can be scary, she holds the steadfast belief that everything will turn out for the better as long as Sonic is by their side.13

Because she loves adventures and fun, Cream has been known to follow Sonic in hopes of him taking her along on his latest adventure. However, she tends to get pouty and upset when Sonic declines her offer to join him in order to protect her from needless danger, as she does not want him to treat her like a little kid. Nonetheless, Cream is always willing to disregard Sonic's warnings and follow him into the heat of danger anyway due to her belief that Sonic can make anything end well.13

Blaze: Sonic... Can you tell me what sort of person he is?

Being inspired by Sonic's trials and battles, Cream originally wished to have the same adventures as him, which means saving the day and defeating evil robots of the Eggman Empire. As such, she tended to feel unfulfilled when she could not participate in the high-octane battles and critical moments Sonic handles. However, thanks to Sonic's words of wisdom, Cream has come to understand that the best kind of adventures to her means being both kind and strong. As long as Cream can handle the kindness, which, as an example, means helping those on the sidelines that get caught in the devastation of villains, while Sonic stands in for the strength, the two of them can still have the best adventures together.13

During the Metal Virus plague, Cream heeded Sonic's advice to be brave after losing Cheese and Chocola. However, when Vanilla fell victim to the Metal Virus, Cream began to lose hope. Later, she showed concern when he did not reappear after disposing of the Metal Virus. When Sonic did return, Cream was happy and joined in on his group hug.
