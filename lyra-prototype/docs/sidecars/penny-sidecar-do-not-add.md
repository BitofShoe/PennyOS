# Sidecar Do Not Add

- Do not replace Penny with Open WebUI, AnythingLLM, LibreChat, Lobe Chat, Onyx, SillyTavern, OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, OpenHands, AutoGPT, or a generic agent OS.
- Do not add n8n, Dify, Flowise, LangFlow, Windmill, or Activepieces to Penny core.
- Do not add MCP gateway/control-plane behavior as a default Penny layer.
- Do not add ambient capture, browser-history capture, continuous screen context, camera history, email automation, social posting, public comms, or cron-like autonomy to Penny core.
- Do not auto-ingest sidecar outputs, docs, web pages, research reports, camera summaries, or Open WebUI knowledge bases into Penny memory.
- Do not enable default thinking, persist hidden reasoning, raise context limits, merge tool evidence into PromptTruth, or expose local endpoints through tunnels by default.
